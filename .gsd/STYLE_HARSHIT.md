> **Origin**: User-provided reference resume (Harshit Kumar)  
> **Status**: Approved Template Model for Template Studio (`template-tech-minimal`)

---

## 1. Typography & Hierarchy
- **Primary Typeface**: Clean, professional sans-serif (Inter / Helvetica / Arial) or modern serif (Merriweather / Georgia) with high legibility and standard character encoding for ATS parsers.
- **Candidate Name**: Centered, uppercase with letter-spacing (`H A R S H I T   K U M A R`), font-size: ~22-24pt, bold/semi-bold.
- **Contact Line**: Centered directly beneath name: `Location • Email • Phone • LinkedIn URL`, font-size: ~9.5-10pt, slate/gray-700.
- **Section Headers**: Uppercase letter-spaced (`S U M M A R Y`, `E X P E R I E N C E`, `S K I L L S`, `E D U C A T I O N`), font-size: ~11-12pt, bold, accented with a 1px solid bottom or top divider line.

---

## 2. Summary Section Layout
- **Style**: Single, high-density narrative paragraph (3–5 lines).
- **Formula**:
  1. Core identity & domain scope (e.g., *"Product Manager building AI-powered consumer and operations experiences at scale..."*).
  2. Intersection of engineering depth + product/consumer intuition.
  3. Signature accomplishments and metrics (ARR, user scale e.g. 8M+ users, GMV, cost reduction).
  4. Positioning closing hook (*"Known for sitting at the intersection of consumer behaviour, AI capability, and business outcomes..."*).

---

## 3. Work Experience Formatting
- **Header Line**: `Role Title • Company Name` on left; `Start Date – End Date` right-aligned. Font-weight: bold for role, regular/medium for company.
- **Bullet Structure (The X-Y-Z Pattern)**:
  - Every bullet begins with a **bolded hook phrase** stating the achievement or action.
  - Followed by specific architecture/methodology and concrete quantified outcome.
  - Examples:
    - `• **Identified that 50% of users were abandoning repair journeys due to rigid triage flows;** redesigned the service triage experience using LLM inference... serving 8M+ annual unique users and cutting abandonment from 50% to 30%.`
    - `• **Designed and shipped Adaptive Support, an AI-powered enterprise platform...** built on LLM inference and RAG-based context retrieval, reduced average handle time by 20% across 10,000 daily agent interactions.`
    - `• **Innovated an industry-first feature achieving one-click data migration...** slashed migration time from 48 hours to just 15 minutes.`

---

## 4. Skills Formatting
- **Layout**: Clean bullet-separated tag rows (`•`) without noisy boxes or bars.
- **Categories**: Product Strategy • AI/ML Product Management • Agentic Systems • LLM Integration & Evaluation • 0→1 Launches • GTM & B2B • Prompt Engineering • Agile/Scrum.

---

## 5. Education Formatting
- Left side: `Degree, Institution Name, Location`
- Right side: `Graduation Years`
